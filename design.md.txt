Design Philosophy for Sumit Kumar Prajapati Portfolio
Design Philosophy
Premium Professional Aesthetic: Clean, fast, results-driven, and trustworthy design language that immediately communicates expertise and reliability.
Color Palette
Primary: Deep charcoal (#1a1a1a) and crisp white (#ffffff)
Accent: Warm gold (#f59e0b) for CTAs and highlights
Secondary: Soft gray (#6b7280) for supporting text
Success: Emerald (#10b981) for positive actions
Background: Consistent light gray (#f8fafc) throughout
Typography
Display Font: "Inter" - Modern, professional sans-serif for headings
Body Font: "Inter" - Consistent typography for readability
Hierarchy: Bold weights for impact, regular for content
Sizing: Large hero text (3rem+), medium section headers (2rem), readable body text (1rem)
Visual Language
Minimalist Approach: Clean layouts with generous white space
Professional Imagery: High-quality portfolio samples and abstract AI visuals
Geometric Elements: Subtle grid patterns and clean lines
Trust Indicators: Client testimonials, project counts, quality badges
Visual Effects & Styling
Used Libraries
Anime.js: Smooth micro-interactions and calculator animations
ECharts.js: Professional data visualization for pricing
Splide.js: Portfolio image carousels and testimonials
Typed.js: Dynamic text effects in hero section
Splitting.js: Text reveal animations
p5.js: Subtle background particle effects
Pixi.js: Interactive visual elements
Animation Strategy
Entrance Animations: Subtle fade-ins and slide-ups on scroll
Hover Effects: 3D transforms on portfolio cards and buttons
Calculator: Real-time price updates with smooth transitions
Loading States: Professional skeleton screens and progress indicators
Header Effect
Gradient Background: Subtle animated gradient with AI-inspired patterns
Floating Elements: Geometric shapes that respond to mouse movement
Parallax Scrolling: Depth effect on hero section
Interactive Elements
Button Hover: Color morphing and subtle shadow expansion
Card Effects: Lift and tilt on hover with shadow depth
Form Focus: Smooth border transitions and input highlighting
Calculator: Dynamic price display with counting animations
Mobile Optimization
Touch Interactions: Larger tap targets and swipe gestures
Responsive Typography: Fluid scaling across devices
Performance: Optimized animations for mobile performance
Accessibility: High contrast ratios and keyboard navigation
